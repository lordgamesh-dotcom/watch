# Design specification

480 × 480 reference canvas, origin at the upper left, center at (240,240), circular black AMOLED background, cyberpunk HUD styling. Palette roles are frame/date, time, heart, battery, steps, stress, hazard/noise, and dim text. The face has an outer tick ring and colored corner arcs, battery readout and segmented bar, weekday/date, large glitchable digital time, technical divider, three circular complication modules for heart rate/steps/stress, and a hazard stripe. RINGS is the default layout; BARS and MINIMAL are configurable. Ambient display has time-only, time-plus-date, and time-plus-battery modes, with low pixel coverage and no animation.

Reference artwork supplied by the user: [Watch face reference](reference-watchface.png) and the AOD image at `/home/floppys/Imagens/AOD (tela sempre ligada)@2x.png`.
